Simple workflow

From reference to a new story.

The goal is to keep the subject and key ideas while creating original cartoon storytelling—not copying the source video shot-for-shot.

01 · Add sourcePaste a supported video URL or describe the topic yourself.
02 · UnderstandUse accessible captions or a model that can read the supplied video.
03 · ReimagineDraft a new script, original characters and scene prompts in 3D style.
04 · GenerateSend to a configured AI video backend and retrieve its output.
